## Decisions still required

- **Owner:** designate privacy, security, accessibility, incident, and data-request owners; approve retention periods; decide how a guardian contacts FP for an under-13 inquiry.
- **Fiscal sponsor:** confirm exact public sponsor name, donation-receipt language, fee disclosure, charitable-solicitation responsibilities, and escalation contact.
- **Privacy counsel:** determine applicable state/country privacy notices, minor consent rules, request deadlines, lawful bases, international transfers, and whether a separate student/guardian notice is needed.
- **Nonprofit counsel:** review entity naming, fundraising statements, sponsorship terminology, disclaimers, governing law, intellectual property, liability, and dispute provisions.
- **Accessibility specialist:** complete independent WCAG 2.2 AA evaluation with assistive-technology users; assess third-party Clerk surfaces and uploaded documents.
- **Security professional:** perform threat modeling and an authorized security assessment; approve monitoring, incident severity, backup, and restore criteria.

No item in this package is attorney approved, an accessibility certification,
or a security certification.
