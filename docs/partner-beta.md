# Partner Beta Guide

The partner beta helps linked partner organizations test opportunity posting, applicant review, interviews, service hours, and feedback workflows.

## Partner Checklist

- Review linked organization details and notify FP staff about corrections.
- Post or update opportunities for student browsing.
- Review applicants from the partner applicant workspace.
- Update applicant statuses so students and FP staff have current information.
- Record interviews and service hours for accepted students.
- Submit applicant quality and review-usefulness feedback where available.

## Beta Expectations

- Partner users see only linked organization data.
- No public partner self-serve onboarding is added in this stage.
- No payments, sponsorship checkout, external calendar integrations, or automated emails are part of the partner beta.
- Report blockers through the authenticated support page with role, URL, steps, and expected behavior.
